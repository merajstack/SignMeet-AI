# SignMeet AI

### AI-Powered Communication Without Barriers

> SignMeet AI is an accessibility-focused communication platform that brings speech, text, sign-language interaction, and translation together into one seamless experience.

🌐 **Live Demo:** `https://signmeet-ai.vercel.app/`
📦 **Repository:** `https://github.com/merajstack/SignMeet-AI`

---

## Overview

Communication should never be limited by the way a person hears, speaks, or communicates.

Today, accessibility is often fragmented across **human interpreters, captioning services, translation applications, and multiple communication tools**. This creates additional cost, complexity, and dependency for people who simply want to participate in a conversation.

**SignMeet AI** approaches the problem differently.

Instead of building another isolated accessibility feature, SignMeet AI creates a unified **AI communication layer** that connects:

**Speech ↔ Text ↔ Sign ↔ Translation**

The goal is simple:

> **Make communication accessible without making accessibility complicated.**

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Millions of conversations become difficult because existing communication systems are not designed for everyone.

People who are deaf, hard of hearing, or face language barriers may have to rely on:

* Human interpreters
* Expensive captioning services
* Separate translation applications
* Manual transcription
* Multiple disconnected accessibility tools

The technology exists.

**The problem is fragmentation.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

SignMeet AI brings multiple communication modes into a single experience.

### Core capabilities

* 🎙️ **Real-Time Speech-to-Text**

  * Convert spoken conversations into readable captions.

* 💬 **Text-Based Communication**

  * Allow participants to communicate without relying entirely on speech.

* 🔊 **Text-to-Speech**

  * Convert written communication into natural voice output.

*  **Sign-Language Interaction**

  * Enable AI-assisted sign-language communication as part of the conversation flow.

* 🌍 **AI Translation**

  * Bridge language barriers during conversations.

* 📝 **Conversation Transcription**

  * Maintain accessible conversation context and transcripts.

## 🧠 Why SignMeet AI?

Traditional accessibility workflows often look like:

```text
Person
  ↓
Interpreter / Captioning Tool
  ↓
Another Person
  ↓
Translation Tool
  ↓
Another System
```

SignMeet AI aims to simplify this:

```text
              ┌─────────────────┐
Speech ──────►│                 │
Text ────────►│   SIGNMEET AI   │──────► Text
Sign ────────►│   AI Layer      │──────► Voice
Language ────►│                 │──────► Translation
              └─────────────────┘
```

### One conversation.

### Multiple ways to communicate.

### One intelligent layer.
